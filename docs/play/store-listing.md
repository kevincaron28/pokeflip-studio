## Full description (4,000 characters at most)

<!-- full:start -->
Cardstead is a price-check and alert app for trading card collectors in Canada.

CHECK A PRICE
• Search sealed products and cards and see the value in Canadian dollars, with its sources, their date and a confidence level: high, medium or low.
• Sold averages from TCGplayer and Cardmarket, converted at the Bank of Canada rate.
• Price history graphs over 7 days, 30 days or 1 year.

LIVE eBay.ca PRICES
• See what an exact card is listed for on eBay.ca right now: the typical price of matching listings that deliver to Canada, with a link to the cheapest one.
• These are asking prices, not completed sales, and they are always labelled that way.

MY CARDS
• A watchlist of the cards you own or want, in English or French, with printing, condition or grade.
• Set a target price or an "under value" level. Cardstead checks prices once a day while it is open and flags the cards that reach it. Low-confidence prices never trigger an alert.

ALL SETS
• Browse every series and set: set value, top cards, sealed prices and card prices in CA$.
• Booster pack value: is a pack worth more opened or sealed? Shown with its sources, where pull rates are published.

DROPS IN CANADA
• Coming drops: release dates of new sets and sealed products.
• Watch a product with the most you would pay, and choose your province and stores.
• Stock alerts come from checks made while the app is open: automatic checks for supported Canadian online card shops, and your own checks for other stores. Only a store can confirm availability.

YOUR DATA
• No account needed. Your cards and watches stay on your phone unless you sign in with your email for cloud backup.
• No ad networks and no analytics trackers.
• Some links to Amazon.ca may be affiliate links; the app says so on screen when they are.

In English and French.

Prices are informational. They are not appraisals, grades or financial advice. Check the exact card and its condition before you buy or sell.

Cardstead is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Nintendo, Creatures Inc., GAME FREAK inc., The Pokémon Company, eBay, Amazon, TCGplayer or Cardmarket. Card names and images belong to their respective owners.
