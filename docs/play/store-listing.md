## Keyword choice

Source: Google Play web search in Canada (`gl=CA`, `en_CA` and `fr_CA`) on 27 Nov 2026, signed in, so suggestions may be personalised. **No search-volume data**: a suggestion shows that people type something like it, not how often.

Search results for "pokemon card price", "tcg card price canada", "card price checker" and "prix carte pokemon" are mostly **card scanners**. Cardstead has no scanner, so "scanner" is not used. "tcg" alone returns card games.

- **Primary keyword, English: "TCG card price"** (inside the suggestion "tcg card price checker"). In the title, the short description, the first sentence and once more.
- **Primary keyword, French: "prix de cartes TCG"** (close to "prix carte"; the exact words drew no suggestion). In the title, the short description, the first sentence and once more.
- **"Pokémon" is not in the title or short description**, although the most direct suggestions contain it and many competing titles use it. Google's [Intellectual Property](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9888072) policy bars using another party's trademark without permission or in a way that could confuse users, and the [Impersonation](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9888374) policy bars implying a relationship; this project renamed itself for that reason ([PLAY-STORE-READINESS.md](../PLAY-STORE-READINESS.md), section 1). Neither page says a disclaimer is enough.
- **One factual mention in the first paragraph** ("It covers the Pokémon Trading Card Game") so people know which game it is, plus the disclaimer. That is the grey area the readiness note describes, and it is new: the 26 Nov text named the company only in the disclaimer. To avoid it, delete that sentence in both languages.

### Full description (4,000 characters at most; 3235)

<!-- full:start -->
Cardstead is a TCG card price checker made for collectors in Canada. Know what a card is worth in Canadian dollars before you buy, sell or trade, keep an eye on the cards you want, and spot restocks. It covers the Pokémon Trading Card Game, and the app works in English and French.

KNOW WHAT YOUR CARDS ARE WORTH
• Search sealed products and the cards of every listed set by name or number. French names work too.
• Every TCG card price shows in CA$ with its sources, their date and a confidence level, so you know how far to trust it.
• Based on TCGplayer and Cardmarket sold prices, converted at the Bank of Canada rate.
• Follow price history over 7 days, 30 days or 1 year, where history is available.

CHECK A LISTING BEFORE YOU PAY
• See the typical eBay.ca asking price of an exact printing now, with a link to the cheapest matching listing that delivers to Canada.
• Find eBay.ca listings of sought-after cards priced under market value.
• Use Buy or Pass to see if a seller's price for a collection leaves room after fees and shipping.
• eBay.ca figures are asking prices, not completed sales.

TRACK THE CARDS YOU WANT
• Build a watchlist with each card's language, printing, condition or grade.
• Set a target price or an "under value" level and see which ones reach it.
• Cardstead checks prices once a day while it is open. Low-confidence prices never trigger an alert.

FINISH YOUR SETS
• Tick the cards you own and see your progress, the value you have and the cost to complete.
• Count one of each, or every printing in Master set mode, then add the missing ones to your watchlist.
• Compare the value of a booster pack opened or sealed, where pull rates are published.

CATCH RESTOCKS IN CANADA
• See coming release dates for new sets and sealed products.
• Browse restocks seen at Canadian online shops over the last 7 days, with the time of each sighting.
• See the typical shop asking price of a sealed product when at least three checked shops have it in stock.
• Watch a product with the most you would pay, and choose your province and stores.

OPTIONAL ALERTS (SIGN-IN NEEDED)
• Drop alerts: a notification when a shop check sees a sealed product back in stock in your area.
• Price alerts: at most one notification a day when a card reaches your target. For ungraded English Near Mint cards added from a set page, using your cloud backup.
• Shop checks can pause, and alerts can be late or missed. Only a store can confirm availability.

YOUR CARDS STAY YOURS
• No account needed to check prices, keep a watchlist or tick sets.
• Everything you enter stays on your phone unless you sign in by email for cloud backup. No password.
• Export a backup file, or delete your account in the app.
• No ad networks and no analytics trackers.
• Some Amazon.ca links may be affiliate links; the app says so on screen when they are.

Prices are informational. They are not appraisals, grades or financial advice. Check the exact card and its condition before you buy or sell.

Cardstead is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Nintendo, Creatures Inc., GAME FREAK inc., The Pokémon Company, eBay, Amazon, TCGplayer or Cardmarket. Card names and images belong to their respective owners.
