A free Cardstead drops server on Discord

What your three Pokémon alert servers do well and badly, and a plan for a free Cardstead server that Canadian collectors can trust more. Nothing is created yet: the server and the bot are yours to make once you decide below.

Servers read: Pokénnoisseur – Canada, Pokewatch Canada, PokeQuebecRead-only, nothing posted

Short answer: yes, the plan fits Cardstead's goals, with one condition. All three servers win on speed for the big chains, and two of them sell auto-checkout tools. Cardstead can't and shouldn't copy either. It can win on what they get wrong: repeated alerts, marketplace sellers posted as restocks, stale in-store pings, no CAD value check, paywalls, and French and English mixed in one place. The Discord server should launch only after the server-side checker and the confirmation step from the redesign brief are built, so the first alerts people see are right.

2. Where Cardstead can do better

Their weak spotCardstead's answer
Same item alerted again and againOne alert per restock: only on sold-out → in-stock at that store, then a 30-minute cooldown. A repeat edits the first message ("still in stock, 12 min") instead of pinging again.
Marketplace sellers posted as restocksThe seller is a field on every card. Anything not sold by the store, or above 1.3× MSRP in CA$, posts as "Marketplace price, not a restock" without a ping.
Stale in-store pingsEvery alert has a "Wasn't there" button. Two of those mark the alert stale and lower that source's score. A public weekly log shows minutes to alert and false-alert rate per store.
No sense of valueCA$ price against market and MSRP with the verdict Cardstead already computes (Under value, Fair, Over value, Scalper price).
Paywalls and paid speedEverything free, the same speed for everyone.
150 shop channels, or nothing in FrenchChannels by region and chain, pings chosen by region and product type, every alert bilingual, chat split FR / EN.
Alerts don't know your collection"View in Cardstead" on every alert, and an opt-in DM for products on your own Cardstead watchlist under your max price.

Where we will be slower, on purpose

Walmart, Best Buy, EB Games, Costco and Pokémon Center block automated checks and their terms forbid them. Cardstead learns about those drops from the retailers' own emails to the drops inbox and from members, so it will usually be minutes behind servers that check those sites directly. The server should say so in its "how alerts work" channel. Card shops, Indigo and Mastermind (Shopify) are checked every 2 to 5 minutes, which is where Cardstead can match them.

What we won't copy

  • Auto-checkout or auto-buy tools. Retailers cancel bot orders and their terms forbid them; it also contradicts Cardstead's "no bot-protection evasion" rule.
  • Paid raffles. A paid-entry draw for a prize is a lottery; in Québec that may need a licence from the Régie des alcools, des courses et des jeux. Not worth the risk.
  • Paid fast lanes. They split the community and are the main complaint pattern.

Pings members choose

Discord's built-in Onboarding asks three questions when someone joins, and they can change answers any time in "Channels & Roles". No bot needed for this.

  • Region: Montréal, Québec regions, GTA, Ottawa/Ontario, BC, Prairies, Atlantic, Online only.
  • Stores: Pokémon Center, Walmart, Best Buy/EB Games, Costco, Amazon, Indigo/Mastermind, pharmacies and grocery, local card shops.
  • Products: booster boxes, ETBs, bundles and blisters, collections and tins, Japanese and Asian, eBay deals.
  • Language: French or English chat.

An alert pings only people whose store and product roles both match (for a shop: region and product). Channels without a match stay quiet: Discord shows the message but nobody is pinged.

Linked to Cardstead

  • Every alert has View in Cardstead: product page with market history and a Watch button.
  • Opt-in Link my Discord in Cardstead settings, then a DM when your own watch drops under your max price. Off by default.
  • The Drops tab and the website footer show a Join the server link; the server's welcome links back to the app.

  • 6Time: when it was first seen, shown in each reader's own time zone, so readers can judge freshness.
  • 7Open at store: the store page. For Amazon, an Associates-tagged link with the disclosure in the footer; no Amazon price until it comes from Amazon's API (their rule).
  • 8Wasn't there: one tap feeds the false-alert rate; two mark the alert stale.

6. Member reports

For the chains Cardstead can't check (in-store stock, Costco, Pokémon Center queues), members are the fastest source. Reports go through a short form so they can be checked.

Report. /report in Discord, or the same form on the website and in the app: store, city or "online", product picked from Cardstead's catalogue, price, a link or photo.

Hold. The report appears in #report-a-drop without pinging anyone, marked "Waiting for a second member".

Confirm. A second member taps I see it too (from another account at least 7 days old), or a trusted member or moderator confirms. Within 30 minutes, or it expires.

Publish. The bot posts the alert card to the right channel with the roles, marked "Member report, confirmed by 2".

Score. Confirmed reports that hold up raise the reporter's trust; reports that get "Wasn't there" lower it. After about 10 good reports a member gets the Trusted reporter role and their reports post at Medium without waiting.

Reporters get no reward that depends on Amazon links (Amazon forbids incentives), and no reward at all beyond the role, so nobody is paid to post.

7. Moderation and anti-spam

  • Alert channels are read-only. Only the bot posts; members talk in the chat and forum channels.
  • Discord AutoMod (free, built in): block invite links, link shorteners, scam phrases ("free nitro", "DM me to buy") and mass mentions; time out on repeat.
  • Verification level "verified email, member for 10 minutes" before chatting; new accounts can't report for 7 days.
  • No buy/sell/trade at launch. Both bigger servers run one, and it is where scams and vouch disputes live. Revisit with two or more active moderators.
  • Rate limits on the bot: at most one alert per product per store per 30 minutes, at most 20 posts a minute across the server, and the bot obeys Discord's rate-limit headers and backs off on errors.
  • Moderators: you plus two volunteers (one francophone) to start, with a pinned rule list in FR and EN and a /flag command that sends a message to a private mod channel.

How it runs: the existing alert pipeline writes each confirmed alert to an alerts table in Supabase. A small Supabase function sends it to Discord through the bot and stores the message ID, so a repeat edits the message instead of posting again. Slash commands and buttons arrive at a second Supabase function as web requests (Discord's "interactions endpoint"), so no always-on bot server is needed.

Discord's rules for this

  • Bots: 50 requests a second overall, plus per-route limits read from Discord's response headers; repeated errors can get the address banned for a while. Our volume is far below this.
  • Each message holds up to 10 embeds; a webhook that returns "not found" must not be used again.
  • Bot DMs only for members who linked their account and turned DMs on.
  • The bot needs a privacy policy and terms link in its Developer Portal profile; Cardstead's privacy page should add a Discord section (what is stored: Discord ID, chosen roles, reports). Discord's Developer Policy page refused automated reading from here, so read it once before launch.

Amazon Associates

  • Show the disclosure "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases" in #amazon-ca's description and on each Amazon card.
  • Amazon allows its links in messages people opted into; it says nothing specific about Discord servers. Posting them in a public, opt-in alert channel with the disclosure is the careful reading.
  • Prices and availability for Amazon only from Amazon's API, with a time stamp. Until then Amazon cards say "Check price at Amazon".

You do these, with your own accounts

  • Create the server from your Discord account (turn on two-factor first), enable Community so Onboarding and forums work.
  • Create the bot application at discord.com/developers: name, icon, privacy and terms links. Copy the bot token straight into Supabase's secrets yourself; it never goes in chat or the repo.
  • Invite the bot with only: view channels, send messages, embed links, manage messages (to edit its own repeats), use slash commands.
  • Pick two moderators.

After that a session can build the posting function, commands, report flow and channel setup on a feature branch with tests.

10. Decisions for you

  1. Start a free Cardstead Discord server?Recommended: yes, but open it after the server-side shop checker, exact matching and confirmation step are live and have caught a few real drops.Alternatives: open now with card shops only; don't do Discord, keep alerts in the app.
  2. Name and language?Recommended: one bilingual server, "Cardstead Drops", alerts in FR and EN on the same card, chat split into French and English channels.Alternatives: a French server and an English server; English only.
  3. Channels per shop or per region?Recommended: per region (10 channels), with the shop on the card and pings by region and product.Alternative: one channel per shop like Pokénnoisseur (about 150 channels).
  4. How many people confirm a member report?Recommended: a second member, or one Trusted reporter or moderator; 30-minute expiry.Alternatives: moderator approval only (slower); post at once marked "unconfirmed" (faster, noisier).
  5. Amazon links in Discord?Recommended: tagged links with the disclosure, no Amazon price until the API is unlocked.Alternative: link to the Cardstead product page only and keep the tag on the website.
  6. Paid tier, raffles, buy/sell/trade?Recommended: none at launch. Everything free, no raffles, no trading channel until there are moderators for it.Alternative: an optional tip jar with no perks, later.
  7. Personal DMs for your own watches?Recommended: yes, opt-in after linking Discord in Cardstead settings, off by default.Alternative: keep personal alerts in the app only.
  8. Where does the bot run?Recommended: Supabase functions (free, no server to keep on).Alternative: an always-on bot on a small paid server (about US$5/month), only needed for features that watch chat live.
