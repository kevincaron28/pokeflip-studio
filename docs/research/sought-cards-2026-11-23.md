Era rule: "vintage" is everything from Base Set through Sun & Moon (WotC, e-Card, EX, DP, HGSS, BW, XY, SM), following the brief; "modern" is Sword & Shield onward. Celebrations and 30th Celebration Classic Collection reprints are modern.

## How the list was chosen

- Each set's PriceCharting list sorted by highest price (the URL in `demandSourceUrl`) was read on 23 November 2026; only single cards that can be pulled from packs were kept. Staff, prerelease, league, prize-pack, store-stamped and "For Position Only" promos were left out (they rank high on PriceCharting but have no clean TCGplayer match and few eBay listings).
- Five 30th Celebration cards come from the TCGplayer September 2026 top-sellers report (the five best-selling $50+ cards).
- Selection: the top card of every set, the TCGplayer best-sellers and the base card of every graded entry, then the most valuable remaining cards up to 62 modern and 54 vintage raw entries. 65 lower-priced candidates are listed at the end for swapping in.
- Prices: TCGplayer market price from the tcgcsv.com files dated 2026-11-23 for the named printing (`subTypeName`). When TCGplayer had no market price for that printing (mostly 1st Edition and e-Card holos), or it was clearly wrong, the PriceCharting ungraded value read from the set page that day is used (`marketSource: "PriceCharting"`).
- Graded entries (PSA 10 / PSA 9) were added only where the PriceCharting set page shows a large graded premium for a card already at the top of its set. PriceCharting set pages show no date next to graded prices, so `marketUsd` is null for every graded entry, as asked; the value seen is in each entry's `note`.
