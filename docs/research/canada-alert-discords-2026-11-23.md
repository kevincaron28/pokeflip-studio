## Takeaways for Cardstead

- Most local shops are small web stores (many on Shopify): these are where automatic in-app checks are realistic, one public product JSON request per watch at a modest interval.
- Shoppers / London Drugs / Mastermind "local stock" means store-by-store availability by postal code.
- Group shops by province and city so a user can follow "Montreal" or "GTA" instead of picking 40 stores.
