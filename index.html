Beginner buying tips

Booster box

Often contains 36 packs in many standard English sets, but special sets and regions vary. Compare total price and per-pack value.

ETB

Includes packs plus accessories and sometimes promos. Pokémon Center and regular editions must be priced separately.

Booster bundle

Often 6 packs, but verify the sealed product. Compare the landed price per pack with other formats.

Collection boxes, tins and blisters

Promo cards, pack selection and exact release vary. Set alone is insufficient: record the actual product variant.

Price per pack measures acquisition cost, not expected value of opening packs. Opening packs to flip singles is high variance. A sold median is not a guaranteed selling price or an authenticated-product check.

Current limitations

OCR is an aid, not a reliable standalone Pokémon card classifier. It can confuse similar printings, reverse holos, special variants, or collector numbers. Match the card art, set and number before saving. No live market prices are embedded; estimated resale values must be entered manually and independently verified. Card names and images load from TCGdex with internet access; photo OCR downloads Tesseract language data on first use. Cloud syncing is optional and requires email sign-in; this release still needs hosted acceptance testing.

Potential future upgrades: dedicated image recognition, automatic set-symbol detection, continuous camera scanning, barcode labels for boxes, login/cloud sync, and verified price feeds.

Fast checkout tips

Cardstead never stores card numbers and never buys for you: stores cancel bot orders and your payment details belong with the store or your phone's wallet. To be fast, set these up once, ahead of a restock. Then Buy now in Restock Watch lands you on a checkout that is already filled in, and you only confirm.

  • Shop Pay (independent card shops): at any Shopify store choose Shop Pay at checkout, verify with the code they send, and save your card and address. Later carts load with your details.
  • Phone wallet / Chrome autofill: save your card in Google Wallet or Chrome's saved payment methods and keep autofill on; stay signed in to Chrome.
  • Amazon.ca: sign in, set a default card and delivery address, stay signed in on your phone browser.
  • Costco.ca, Walmart.ca, EB Games, Best Buy, Canadian Tire, Toys"R"Us, Indigo, London Drugs, The Source, Shoppers, Mastermind, Well.ca: create an account, save a card and address in its wallet or payment settings, and stay signed in on your phone browser.
  • Pokémon Center and other queue drops: sign in before the drop; queues cannot be skipped.
  • Limits: many shops cap quantity per customer; enter that limit on the watch so Buy now never asks for more.
  • In the Android app checkout opens in your browser, so your saved logins and autofill apply.
