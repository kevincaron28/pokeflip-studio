Cardstead Drop Assist

Watches the Amazon.ca and Pokémon Center Canada products you add. When one is in stock at or under your max price, it alerts you loudly, adds your quantity to the cart and opens checkout with your saved address and payment. You click "Place order" yourself. It never stores passwords or card numbers, never solves a CAPTCHA and never skips the Pokémon Center line.
